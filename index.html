Device User Guide

Hardware Diagram

  • Orange Ear Buttons: Twist left ear to adjust volume. Twist right ear to skip track list. Press both ears to enter Wi-Fi mode.
  • Card Slot: Vertically slot cards facing forwards to immediately play. Remove card to pause.
  • USB-C Dock: Charge by setting Cheeko onto the circular magnetic charging dock base.
  • Headphone Jack: Standard 3.5mm jack on the rear for quiet playback.

LED Indicators

  • Pulsing Blue Eyes: Device is currently downloading cards or syncing.
  • Solid White Eyes: Cheeko is active and ready.
  • Soft Green Eyes: AI assistant mic is listening to child replies.
  • Pulsing Red Eyes: Low battery warning (below 15%).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cheeko completely screen-free? ▼

Yes! Cheeko features zero LED screens. Feedback is given through friendly voice prompts, spatial audio soundscapes, and soft light pulses in the device's ears.

Do the audio cards need a Wi-Fi connection to play? ▼

No, once standard card audio files have synced to Cheeko, they store directly on the device memory (8GB storage) and play offline completely. Only AI conversation sessions require a network link.

What ages is Cheeko suitable for? ▼

Cheeko is designed for kids aged 2 to 10. Our card catalog divides ranges into Ages 0-2, 3-5, 6-8, and 9+ with age-appropriate storylines, cognitive math puzzles, and mentor dialogues.
